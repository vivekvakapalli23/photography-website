About Me

I'm Vivek

My journey behind the lens began with a simple fascination: how a single, fleeting second of light could be caught and saved forever. Over the last five years, this fascination has matured into a dedication to premium visual storytelling.

I believe that luxury photography isn't just about high-resolution equipment; it's about anticipation, composition, and emotional resonance. Whether framing a quiet look between a bride and groom or capturing the dynamic energy of a major celebration, my team and I work to reveal the absolute beauty of every scene.
